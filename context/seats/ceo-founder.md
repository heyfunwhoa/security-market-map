## Objections

- "Security doesn't move revenue." Usually about: Security is seen as overhead. Reframe to test: Tie it to deals blocked by security reviews or to insurance and audit requirements. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Is security slowing revenue or fundraising? Good fit if: Security reviews in deals or investor diligence. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Is there no security owner yet? Good fit if: The founder or CTO covers security. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is the company too early to have customers or data? Poor fit if: No production data. (medium weight, hypothesis)
