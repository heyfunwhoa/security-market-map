## Objections

- "Our platform vendor already does this." Usually about: Fear of tool sprawl and of explaining another line item. Reframe to test: Compare what the bundled feature covers today against the sources and depth this problem needs, then decide whether the gap is worth a separate tool. — hypothesis
- "It's not in this year's budget." Usually about: No clear owner for the spend, or the problem hasn't been tied to a risk the board tracks. Reframe to test: Find whose existing budget this replaces or protects, and tie the problem to an exposure already on the risk register. — sourced
- "You haven't passed our security review yet." Usually about: Procurement or legal needs paperwork before anyone will sign. Reframe to test: Send the security pack, DPA, and data-flow summary early so the review runs in parallel with evaluation. — hypothesis
- "We need multi-year terms for that price." Usually about: Procurement wants a discount and fewer renewals. Reframe to test: Trade term length for price protection and an exit tied to agreed success measures. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Does vendor onboarding include a security review step? Good fit if: Security questionnaires before contract. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is there a renewal date for an overlapping tool? Good fit if: A contract end date within a year. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Is the company in a buying freeze? Poor fit if: A freeze on new vendors. (high weight, hypothesis)
