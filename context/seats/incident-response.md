# Incident responder

Investigate, analyze, and respond to incidents; contain.

## Typical titles

- ic: Incident Response Analyst, IR Analyst
- senior-ic: Senior Incident Response Analyst, Lead Incident Response Analyst
- manager: Incident Response Manager, IR Manager
- director: Director of Incident Response, Head of Incident Response

## Owns categories

- SOAR and response automation (`soar`)
- SIEM (`siem`)
- Extended detection and response (`xdr`)
- Managed detection and response (`mdr`)
- Endpoint security (`endpoint-security`)
- Identity threat detection (ITDR) (`itdr`)

## Pains

- Unclear authority to contain — hypothesis
- Third-party incidents with little visibility — sourced ([Verizon](https://www.verizongov.com/resources/reports/2025-dbir-executive-summary.pdf))

## Objections

- "Our IR retainer covers this." Usually about: Retainers feel like insurance for the whole problem. Reframe to test: Check what the retainer includes, how fast it starts, and what the internal team still has to do in the first hours. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Has the company had an incident in the last 18 months? Good fit if: A disclosed or internal incident. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Is containment authority unclear during incidents? Good fit if: Delays waiting on approvals. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is IR fully handled by a retainer with no internal responder? Poor fit if: No internal IR role. (medium weight, hypothesis)
