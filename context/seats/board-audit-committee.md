# Board / audit or risk committee

Oversee cyber risk; hold management accountable.

## Typical titles

- c-level: Board, Audit committee, Risk committee, Board of directors

## Pains

- Personal accountability for cyber oversight in regulated sectors — sourced ([Springlex](https://www.springlex.eu/en/packages/nis2/nis2-directive/article-20/); [Hannes Snellman](https://digitalhorizon.hannessnellman.com/?p=5896))
- Hard to judge security posture from management reports — hypothesis

## Objections

- "The board needs a simple answer, not more detail." Usually about: Directors can't judge technical reports. Reframe to test: Offer one or two measures the board can track over time, tied to risks already on the register. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Does the board review cyber risk at least quarterly? Good fit if: A standing cyber agenda item. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is the company public or heavily regulated? Good fit if: SEC, NYDFS, DORA or sector oversight. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Does the board have no cyber oversight assigned? Poor fit if: No committee owns cyber. (low weight, hypothesis)

## Buying triggers
