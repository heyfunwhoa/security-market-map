# Privacy / DPO

Run the privacy program: lawful processing, subject rights, DPIAs, and customer DPA evidence.

## Typical titles

- ic: Privacy Analyst, Data Protection Analyst
- senior-ic: Senior Privacy Analyst, Lead Privacy Analyst
- manager: Privacy Manager, Data Protection Manager
- director: Director of Privacy, Head of Privacy
- vp: VP of Privacy
- c-level: CPO, Chief Privacy Officer

## Owns categories

- Privacy governance (`privacy-governance`)
- Data security posture (`dspm`)
- Data loss prevention (`dlp`)
- Customer trust (`customer-trust`)

## Objections

- "You haven't passed our security review yet." Usually about: Procurement or legal needs paperwork before anyone will sign. Reframe to test: Send the security pack, DPA, and data-flow summary early so the review runs in parallel with evaluation. — hypothesis
- "We can't give a vendor access to that data." Usually about: Privacy, residency, or contract limits on sharing code or customer data. Reframe to test: Clarify what leaves the environment, what is stored, and whether a self-hosted or scoped option exists. — hypothesis
- "Legal and HR won't let us monitor employees that closely." Usually about: Privacy, labor law, and trust concerns. Reframe to test: Bring legal and HR in early, scope monitoring to risk signals, and agree on who sees what. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Is the company subject to GDPR, state privacy laws, or sector rules with data requests? Good fit if: DSARs, records of processing, or regional data rules. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Are AI or new data uses outpacing privacy reviews? Good fit if: A backlog of privacy reviews. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is privacy handled entirely by outside counsel with no internal owner? Poor fit if: No DPO or privacy lead. (medium weight, hypothesis)
