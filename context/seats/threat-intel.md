# Threat intelligence analyst

Collect, analyze, and disseminate threat assessments and indicators.

## Typical titles

- ic: Threat Intelligence Analyst, Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst
- senior-ic: Senior Threat Intelligence Analyst, Lead Threat Intelligence Analyst
- manager: Threat Intelligence Manager, Cyber Threat Intelligence Manager
- director: Director of Threat Intelligence, Head of Threat Intelligence

## Pains

- Intelligence requirements and collection plans are rarely formalized — sourced ([SANS Institute](https://socradar.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/SANS-2025-CTI-Cyber_Threat_Intelligence_Survey-SOCRadar.pdf))

- Hard to show value to the teams who consume it — hypothesis

## Objections

- "We already get more intel than we can use." Usually about: Feeds don't turn into actions. Reframe to test: Judge intel by how many detections, blocks, or decisions it produced last quarter. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Does intel feed detections, blocks or executive decisions? Good fit if: Intel-driven detections or briefings. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is the company in a heavily targeted sector? Good fit if: Finance, healthcare, government contractors, critical infrastructure. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is intel limited to free feeds with no analyst? Poor fit if: No CTI role. (low weight, hypothesis)
