Approve spend and terms; decide budget timing.

- manager: Finance Manager, FP&A Manager

## Objections

- "It's not in this year's budget." Usually about: No clear owner for the spend, or the problem hasn't been tied to a risk the board tracks. Reframe to test: Find whose existing budget this replaces or protects, and tie the problem to an exposure already on the risk register. — sourced
- "We need multi-year terms for that price." Usually about: Procurement wants a discount and fewer renewals. Reframe to test: Trade term length for price protection and an exit tied to agreed success measures. — hypothesis
- "Security doesn't move revenue." Usually about: Security is seen as overhead. Reframe to test: Tie it to deals blocked by security reviews or to insurance and audit requirements. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Is security spend under review or consolidation? Good fit if: A budget review or vendor rationalization. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Do insurance premiums depend on specific controls? Good fit if: Insurer requirements for MFA, EDR or backups. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is the purchase below the CFO's approval threshold? Poor fit if: A small deal that the CFO won't see. (low weight, hypothesis)
