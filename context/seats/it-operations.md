## Pains

- Edge device patches take weeks, and many never fully land — sourced ([HIPAA Journal](https://www.hipaajournal.com/verizon-dbir-2025/))
- Every security tool adds agents and tickets — hypothesis
- Too few people for patching and access requests — hypothesis

## Objections

- "Our backups are fine." Usually about: Backups exist, but recovery hasn't been tested under attack. Reframe to test: Ask when a full restore was last tested, and whether the attacker's credentials could reach the backups. — hypothesis
- "My team will end up supporting this." Usually about: Security tools create tickets and agents for IT. Reframe to test: Ask who deploys, maintains, and answers tickets, and agree on it before buying. — hypothesis
- "HR data isn't clean enough to drive access." Usually about: Joiner, mover and leaver events in HR are late or wrong. Reframe to test: Start with leavers and contractors, where late changes create the most risk. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Will IT deploy and support the tool? Good fit if: IT owns agents and endpoint deployment. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Are patching and access requests backlogged? Good fit if: Ticket queues growing. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is IT fully outsourced? Poor fit if: An MSP runs all IT operations. (low weight, hypothesis)
