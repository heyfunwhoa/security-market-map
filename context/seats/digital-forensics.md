# Digital forensics

Preserve and analyze evidence so it survives legal and regulatory scrutiny.

## Typical titles

- ic: Digital Forensics Analyst, Forensics Analyst
- senior-ic: Senior Digital Forensics Analyst, Lead Digital Forensics Analyst
- manager: Digital Forensics Manager, Forensics Manager
- director: Director of Digital Forensics, Head of Digital Forensics

## Owns categories

- Endpoint security (`endpoint-security`)
- SIEM (`siem`)
- Extended detection and response (`xdr`)
- Insider risk (`insider-risk`)
- File and malware analysis (`malware-analysis`)

## Pains

- The fastest intrusions reach data theft in about an hour, before an investigation can start — sourced ([Cloud Security Alliance](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-ai-compressed-exposure-window-20260720-csa/))
- Evidence lost during fast containment — hypothesis
- Retainer scope doesn't match the incident — hypothesis

## Objections

- "Our IR retainer covers this." Usually about: Retainers feel like insurance for the whole problem. Reframe to test: Check what the retainer includes, how fast it starts, and what the internal team still has to do in the first hours. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Does the company need defensible evidence for legal or regulatory reasons? Good fit if: Litigation, regulators or insurance claims. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Has evidence been lost during containment before? Good fit if: Reimaged systems before collection. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is forensics fully covered by an outside retainer? Poor fit if: No internal forensics capability. (medium weight, hypothesis)
