# Insider risk

Run a people-risk program and its cases, often with HR and legal.

## Typical titles

- ic: Insider Risk Analyst, Insider Threat Analyst
- senior-ic: Senior Insider Risk Analyst, Lead Insider Risk Analyst
- manager: Insider Risk Manager, Insider Threat Manager
- director: Director of Insider Risk, Head of Insider Risk

## Owns categories

- Insider risk (`insider-risk`)
- Data loss prevention (`dlp`)
- Endpoint security (`endpoint-security`)
- Email security (`email-security`)

## Pains

- Insider incidents are costly and take months to contain — sourced ([Ponemon Institute](https://ponemon.dtexsystems.com))
- Privacy and HR limits on monitoring — hypothesis
- Cases cut across HR, legal, and security — hypothesis

## Objections

- "Legal and HR won't let us monitor employees that closely." Usually about: Privacy, labor law, and trust concerns. Reframe to test: Bring legal and HR in early, scope monitoring to risk signals, and agree on who sees what. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Does the company hold IP or customer data that departing staff could take? Good fit if: Source code, customer lists or trade secrets. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Are legal and HR already engaged on insider cases? Good fit if: A cross-functional insider risk group. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Do works councils or local law block monitoring outright? Poor fit if: Legal has said no to monitoring. (medium weight, hypothesis)
