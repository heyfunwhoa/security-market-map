## Objections

- "Business teams own those SaaS apps, not us." Usually about: No authority over app settings or integrations. Reframe to test: Agree on a shared baseline and route fixes to app owners with clear risk, instead of taking over admin. — hypothesis
- "We can't risk downtime on the plant floor." Usually about: Safety and production come before security. Reframe to test: Start with passive visibility that doesn't touch devices, and plan changes inside maintenance windows. — hypothesis
- "Our backups are fine." Usually about: Backups exist, but recovery hasn't been tested under attack. Reframe to test: Ask when a full restore was last tested, and whether the attacker's credentials could reach the backups. — hypothesis
- "My team will end up supporting this." Usually about: Security tools create tickets and agents for IT. Reframe to test: Ask who deploys, maintains, and answers tickets, and agree on it before buying. — hypothesis

## Qualification questions

- Does IT own security budget or tool support? Good fit if: Security tools on the IT budget or support roster. (high weight, hypothesis)
- Is tool consolidation a stated goal? Good fit if: A vendor rationalization effort. (medium weight, hypothesis)
- Is IT frozen on a major migration? Poor fit if: An ERP or cloud migration taking all capacity. (medium weight, hypothesis)
