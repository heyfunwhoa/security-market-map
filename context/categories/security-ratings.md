# Security ratings

Outside-in scores of a company's exposed posture, used in third-party risk and insurance conversations. This map keeps a score separate from an assessment the supplier filled out, and separate from a penetration test.

**Buyer question:** Whose outside-in score are we using, and what decision does it actually change?

**Usual owner:** Third-party risk.

**Budget source:** Third-party risk or cyber insurance.

## Owning seats

- GRC or trust lead (`grc-owner`)

## Vendors (primary)

- BitSight: Security ratings vendor used in third-party risk. A score is not a questionnaire and not a pentest.
- SecurityScorecard: Security ratings vendor on the same shortlist as BitSight. No scoring method is sourced here.
- Black Kite: Third-party security ratings candidate. Unverified.

## Vendors (adjacent)

_None recorded._

## What buyers do instead

- Questionnaires and SOC 2 reports only. Good enough when: A short vendor list reviewed yearly. Breaks when: Continuous monitoring or board reporting on third-party risk is required. — hypothesis
- No outside-in scoring. Good enough when: Customers and insurers don't ask. Breaks when: An insurer, board, or customer starts quoting a rating at you. — hypothesis

## Objections

- "The board needs a simple answer, not more detail." — hypothesis

## Triggers

- A supplier questionnaire pile
- A carrier asking for a score
