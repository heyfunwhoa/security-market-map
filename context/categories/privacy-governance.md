# Privacy governance

Records of processing, consent, data-subject requests, and privacy reviews. Legal and privacy own it. Security supplies the technical controls. Do not sell a scanner as a privacy program.

**Buyer question:** Can we say what personal data we hold and why?

**Usual owner:** Privacy counsel or a privacy office.

**Budget source:** Privacy. Separate from DSPM unless the buyer connects them.

## Owning seats

- Data security (`data-security`)
- Privacy / DPO (`privacy`)
- GRC or trust lead (`grc-owner`)

## Vendors (primary)

- OneTrust: Privacy and third-party risk platform. Privacy governance is not DSPM.
- Securiti: Securiti markets a data security posture management solution. Vendor-reported positioning reviewed 2026-10-13; feature depth and effectiveness remain untested.

## What buyers do instead

- Records of processing and DSARs tracked in spreadsheets. Good enough when: Low request volume and one main regulation. Breaks when: Requests, data maps, and regulations multiply across regions. — hypothesis
- Outside counsel runs privacy compliance. Good enough when: Few systems and infrequent changes. Breaks when: Product changes outpace reviews and costs climb. — hypothesis
