# AI governance

Inventory of AI systems, the policy that applies to them, and the audit trail. Governance records the decision. It does not, by itself, stop a tool call or scan a prompt.

**Buyer question:** Which AI systems are approved, who owns them, and where is the record?

**Usual owner:** AI governance, risk, or the CISO's office.

**Budget source:** GRC or a new AI-governance line.

## What buyers do instead

- An acceptable-use policy and a review committee. Good enough when: Few AI tools and low regulatory exposure. Breaks when: Teams adopt AI tools faster than reviews run, or regulation requires inventories. — hypothesis
