# Third-party risk

Assessments of vendors and subprocessors. Related to customer trust when you are the vendor being assessed. The questionnaire is not a technical test of their product.

**Buyer question:** Which suppliers can affect us, and what did we actually review?

**Usual owner:** Third-party risk or procurement security.

**Budget source:** GRC or procurement.

## Owning seats

- GRC or trust lead (`grc-owner`)

## Who vendors sell to

- None recorded.

## Vendors (primary)

- OneTrust: Privacy and third-party risk platform. Privacy governance is not DSPM.

## Vendors (adjacent)

- BitSight
- SecurityScorecard
- Black Kite
- Credo AI

## What buyers do instead

- Questionnaires by email. Good enough when: A short vendor list reviewed once a year. Breaks when: Hundreds of vendors, continuous monitoring, or a third-party breach. — hypothesis

## Objections

_None recorded._

## Triggers

- New subprocessor
- A supplier incident
- An EU financial entity works through DORA ICT risk-management requirements.

_Vendor positioning is vendor-reported unless a claim says otherwise. A shared category is not proof of direct competition._
