# Security awareness

Training and simulated phishing for people. It changes behavior. It does not replace email security or identity threat detection.

**Buyer question:** Will people report the message, and is the program more than an annual video?

**Usual owner:** Security awareness, often under the CISO.

**Budget source:** Awareness, a small line next to email and identity.

## Owning seats

- IT / corporate security (`it-security`)
- Security awareness / people security (`security-awareness`)

## Vendors (primary)

- GhostEye: New York startup, Y Combinator Summer 2025. Sells "vulnerability management for the human layer": maps what attackers can learn about employees and runs personalized social engineering attacks over email, SMS and voice, including against help desks.
