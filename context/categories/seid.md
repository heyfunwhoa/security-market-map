# Social Engineering Identity Defense (SEID)

Protects sensitive identity workflows such as help desk resets, account recovery and onboarding from impersonation. Common controls include identity proofing at the point of change, approvals and an audit trail.

**Buyer question:** How do we verify someone before resetting access or changing a recovery factor?

**Usual owner:** Workforce IAM and the service desk

**Budget source:** Validate against the team responsible for the workflow.
