# OT and IoT security

Visibility and detection for operational technology and unmanaged devices. This map keeps that work apart from corporate endpoint detection, because many of these assets cannot run an endpoint agent.

**Buyer question:** What is on the plant or device network, and what is it talking to?

**Usual owner:** OT security, with operations. Not the laptop team by default.

**Budget source:** OT security or a plant budget. A corporate EDR renewal does not cover it.

## What buyers do instead

- Air gaps and network segmentation. Good enough when: Isolated plants with little remote access. Breaks when: Remote access, IT/OT convergence, or a regulator needs visibility into OT traffic. — hypothesis
- Extend IT security tools into OT. Good enough when: A few OT assets that behave like IT systems. Breaks when: Industrial protocols and fragile devices can't take IT agents or scans. — hypothesis

## Objections

- "We can't risk downtime on the plant floor." — hypothesis
