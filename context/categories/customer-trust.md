# Customer trust

Questionnaires, penetration-test letters, and control descriptions a buyer of your product asks for. Related to GRC. The audience is the customer's security review, not your own corporate audit.

**Buyer question:** Can we answer a customer's security review without inventing controls?

**Usual owner:** Trust or product security, with GRC if the company is large.

**Budget source:** Trust or compliance automation. Enterprise GRC is a different scale.

## Owning seats

- Privacy / DPO (`privacy`)
- GRC or trust lead (`grc-owner`)

## Vendors (primary)

- Vanta: Customer-trust and compliance automation often bought by smaller companies. Not an enterprise GRC suite by default.

## Vendors (adjacent)

- HackerOne

## What buyers do instead

- Security team answers questionnaires by hand. Good enough when: A few enterprise deals a quarter. Breaks when: Questionnaires block deals and pull engineers off roadmap work. — hypothesis

## Objections

- "Auditors won't accept tool output as evidence." — hypothesis
- "Security doesn't move revenue." — hypothesis

## Triggers

- Enterprise deal blocked on security review
- SOC 2 or ISO project
- The company needs its first SOC 2 or ISO 27001 to close enterprise deals.
- Security questionnaires start blocking or slowing enterprise deals.
- A large funding round sets expectations for enterprise readiness.
