# Digital risk protection

A search for public abuse of a brand, domain, executive, product, or customer-facing asset the organization already knows. That search is not an inventory of unknown internet-facing hosts, and it is not a missing-patch scan.

**Buyer question:** Which public abuse of a known brand is in scope, and who owns the asset being abused?

**Usual owner:** Brand protection or the security team that handles external abuse. Often not the vulnerability-scanning owner.

**Budget source:** Brand protection or corporate security. Confirm it is not being bought as an attack-surface scanner.
