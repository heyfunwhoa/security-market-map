# Cyber asset management

A consolidated inventory of devices, accounts, and cloud assets from the tools that already see them. This map uses cyber asset attack surface management for that inventory. It is the list. It is not the scanner and not the EDR.

**Buyer question:** Can we name every asset, and which tool is the source of truth?

**Usual owner:** Security operations or IT asset management.

**Budget source:** Security operations. Often justified as the layer under exposure management.

## What buyers do instead

- CMDB plus spreadsheets. Good enough when: A stable estate and a disciplined IT team. Breaks when: Cloud, SaaS, and shadow IT make the CMDB wrong within weeks. — hypothesis
- Inventory views in existing EDR and cloud consoles. Good enough when: Most assets are covered by one or two tools. Breaks when: Nobody can answer what exists across all tools, or what's unmanaged. — hypothesis
