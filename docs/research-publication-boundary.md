# Public research publication boundary

This repository is a **public, company- and market-focused research resource**. Apply the same separation used for Infisical-related research to every vendor, opportunity, and competitive brief.

## Permitted here
- Publicly verifiable company facts, positioning, products, pricing, customers publicly identified by the company, category mapping, integrations, and competitors.
- URLs, publication dates, evidence confidence, and explicit uncertainty.
- Generic buyer personas, sales motions, and category-level evaluation frameworks that are not tied to an individual's job search.

## Never publish here
- Personal identities or contact details, private Gmail messages, recruiter correspondence, personal job-search status, interview schedules or notes, salary expectations, compensation negotiations, resumes, private account information, or private assessments of whether an individual should take a role.
- Nonpublic employer/customer information, internal deal intelligence, personal notes, internal linked records, or private repository content.
- Direct links to private Gmail threads, private Notion pages, or other private workspaces.

## Publication procedure
1. Classify source as public or private before adding it.
2. Research a company's public information independently instead of laundering claims from a private recruiter email into a public vendor entry.
3. Keep personal opportunity analysis in a private repository or private interview command center only.
4. Review each public-facing PR for personal data, private URLs, attribution leakage, and unsupported assertions before merging.
5. Prefer a minimal, generalized market research summary if the distinction is ambiguous; otherwise omit the content until approved.

This boundary applies to newly created research and updates of existing material. No private work should be copied into this repository simply because it is useful for career evaluation.
