# Decision brief: technology consolidation

Status: educational candidate; human review pending. Owner: Market Map maintainer. Version: 0.1. Updated: 2026-11-06. Review due: 2026-12-04. No actual contracts, discounts or savings are asserted.

## Decision and outcome

Decide whether to retire, retain or replace overlapping technology around one defined workflow. Desired outcome: lower avoidable total cost while preserving required service, coverage and operational flexibility. Capability: understand usage, compare requirements and execute a safe transition.

Illustrative trigger: a renewal or budget review. Actual renewal date, cancellation window and urgency: Unknown. A smaller vendor count alone is not the business outcome.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

| Alternative | Potential value | Cost / limitation | Disqualifying evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retain and right-size | Lower disruption | Some overlap remains | Material waste cannot be removed this way |
| Expand incumbent and retire another tool | Simplify operation | Migration and supplier concentration | Required coverage cannot be demonstrated |
| Replace with a specialist or different platform | Better requirements fit | Integration and procurement effort | Total cost/transition risk exceeds benefit |
| Build an integration or narrow capability | Tailored consolidation | Ongoing engineering dependency | No sustainable maintenance owner |
| Outsource part of the workflow | Change staffing/tool model | Service obligations and retained costs | Contract or operational boundaries do not meet needs |
| Defer retirement | Avoid unsafe migration | Continued duplicate cost | Unacceptable ongoing business impact |

## Dependencies and failure modes

First inventory actual contracts, usage, owners, required functions, data exports and dependencies. Sequence validation before decommissioning. Include notice periods, termination costs, retention obligations, retraining and parallel operation.

Failure modes: counting unused licenses as removable when minimum commitments remain, counting capacity as cash, retiring unique coverage, losing historical data, or replacing software expense with more labor. Roll back within the validated transition window if requirements fail. Do not retire the old capability solely because the new license is purchased.
