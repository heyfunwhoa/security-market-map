## Boundaries

These original links support vendor-reported positioning only; no effectiveness, pricing or parity is verified. Publication dates are unknown unless supplied by the original; Microsoft displays an update date of 2026-05-26, which is not assumed to be initial publication. Rubrik's evidence is a vendor-published customer story, not independent validation.

Cyera and ten other parent vendors already existed: refresh rather than duplicate. Metomic is new. Existing Teleskope, Concentric AI and Nightfall research remains in [the earlier brief](dspm-vendor-refresh.md). Broader vendors retain their existing primary categories; DSPM is added as an adjacent offering where missing. Product packaging should be checked separately from the parent company.

Normalyze, Eureka and Theom remain catalog candidates requiring current ownership/packaging review; do not count historical brands as independently purchasable alternatives without checking. Laminar is identified as a former Rubrik DSPM name by the cited source, not added as a separate vendor.
