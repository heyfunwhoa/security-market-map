## Editorial rules

Record publication date separately from report edition and retrieval date. Search-engine crawl dates are not publication dates. Retain survey fieldwork, sample, geography, method and question wording when available; otherwise keep Unknown. Preserve prior forecast vintages. Do not convert expectations into actuals, category adoption into spend, or framework guidance into purchase mandates. Use short paraphrases; no redistribution of report PDFs or analyst graphics.
