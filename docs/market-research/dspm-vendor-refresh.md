## Comparison boundaries
- Partial overlap is a research hypothesis, not a documented head-to-head shortlist. No direct-competitor relationships added.
- Alternatives include expanding an incumbent data-security platform, retaining current controls, or a scoped internal discovery process. Compare coverage, operational ownership and cost before selecting.
- Unknown: prices, contract entitlements, deployment architecture, independently measured accuracy and remediation effectiveness.
- Validate with a bounded test set: discovery coverage, access-risk accuracy, approved remediation/rollback, prevention behavior and operator effort.
- No unsupported superiority claims, accuracy percentages or roadmap availability promoted.
