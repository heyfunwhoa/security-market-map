# LimaCharlie ICP trigger signals and validation evidence

As of 2026-10-29. **Candidate research only**: these are hypotheses and qualification patterns, not observed LimaCharlie buying behavior, confirmed intent, a scoring algorithm, or published catalog claims. Companion [audiences](limacharlie-audience-personas.md), [environment profiles](limacharlie-environments-sales-cycles-2026-10-29.md), and [GTM Revenue OS outbound model](https://github.com/heyfunwhoa/gtm-revenue-os/blob/main/docs/product/outbound-gtm-operating-model.md).

## Qualify five independently evidenced dimensions

1. **Operational pain:** specific recurring queue, tenant-onboarding effort, detection gap, integration cost or delayed incident response. Seek baseline and owner.
2. **Technical fit:** representative telemetry, adapter and permission scope, D&R rule, governance, audit and realistic negative cases. A capability claim does not establish integration-specific fit.
3. **Economics:** fully loaded labor, endpoint and external-GB meters, AI/outputs and engineering cost versus incremental service revenue or measured savings; validate pricing terms.
4. **Urgency:** funded initiative, renewal, new service launch or defined project date. Headcount growth alone is not urgency.
5. **Expansion:** documented path from one paid workload to more tenants, workloads or usage with acceptable marginal operating costs.

## Evidence ladder

- **Weak:** job postings, funding, generic platform usage, vendor logos. These indicate possible fit, not purchase intent.
- **Moderate:** stated services expansion, vendor-stack migration, renewal window, named operating problem.
- **Strong:** quantified workflow pain, accountable owner, funded timeline, agreed technical scope and measurable trial.
- **Confirmed:** successful safe production deployment, actual paid usage and observed customer expansion. Record date, source, exclusions and whether evidence is internal/private. Never publish identifiable customer/private interviews into public research.

## Stage-gated validation and falsifiers

Discovery: pain + owner + trigger. Reject if no meaningful problem. Technical: one integration with both success and failure/approval tests. Reject if permissions or coverage fail. Economic: compare *all* meters and support hours; reject if marginal economics don't work. Production: actual paid usage vs trial; reject if deployment is not repeatable. Expansion: additional tenant/service consumption with retention and acceptable marginal support load; don't treat projected usage as realized ARR.

Suggested measurements: workflow-qualified conversations / accounts approached; percent of scoped evaluations reaching paid production; median days to first paid usage; 30/60/90-day realized consumption; added paid tenants; implementation hours; support burden; qualified outcomes with cohort/sample-size caveats.
